06 / CLAIM BOUNDARY

有说服力，也不越过证据

面试项目最重要的不是把每个输出叫作“因果”，而是知道哪些结论能由当前数据支持，哪些必须拒绝。

SUPPORTED

当前项目可以证明

  • Chronological out-of-time prediction among the retained accepted-loan cohort
  • Strict rejection of post-outcome fields from model inputs and generated programs
  • Executable model-scenario queries with complete audit traces
NOT IDENTIFIED

当前项目明确不声称

  • Causal treatment effects of changing loan amount, DTI, or utilization
  • Reject inference for applicants who were never funded
  • Autonomous real-world approval or adverse-action decisions
